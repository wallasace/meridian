# Commercial licensing

Meridian is free to run and modify for internal purposes, under the
[PolyForm Internal Use License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Some uses fall outside that
license and need a separate agreement.

This page explains which is which, and how to ask.

> **Not legal advice.** The definitions below are written to make the intent
> readable. The binding text is the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file. A commercial
> agreement, if we reach one, is a separate document and should be reviewed by
> your legal team and mine.

## What the terms mean here

These words are often used loosely. Within this project they mean:

| Term | Meaning in this project |
|---|---|
| **Personal use** | An individual running Meridian on their own devices, for any reason. |
| **Educational use** | Running, reading or modifying Meridian to learn or teach — a course, a study group, a workshop. |
| **Internal business use** | You, or the organization you work for, running and modifying Meridian for your own operations. Your colleagues using it to check teammates' hours is exactly this. |
| **Collaboration** | Forking the repository to propose a change back, and discussing that change publicly. Permitted by an additional grant in [LICENSE](LICENSE). |
| **Distribution** | Passing Meridian — source or built app — to anyone outside your organization. |
| **Redistribution** | Publishing Meridian, changed or not, for others to obtain: an app store, a download page, a package registry, a bundled installer. |
| **Commercial use** | Any use whose purpose is to generate revenue for someone other than the licensor, beyond internal business use as defined above. |
| **Incorporation into a commercial product** | Embedding Meridian, in whole or in part, in software or hardware that is sold, licensed or offered to customers. |
| **SaaS / network service** | Running Meridian so that people outside your organization reach it over a network, whether or not they pay. |

## No licence needed

- Running Meridian on your own machine
- Your team or your whole company running it internally
- Reading the code, and learning from it
- Modifying it for your own internal use
- Forking to send a pull request

Internal business use is already granted by the license, at no cost, for
organizations of any size.

## Commercial licence needed

- Selling Meridian, or charging for access to it
- Shipping it inside a product or service offered to your customers
- Hosting it as a service reachable from outside your organization
- Redistributing it through a store, registry or installer
- Removing or replacing the Meridian attribution in a distributed build

## How to ask

Open an issue titled **"Commercial licence enquiry"** at
<https://github.com/wallasace/meridian/issues>, or reach the maintainer through
the profile at <https://github.com/wallasace>.

Useful things to say up front:

1. Who the organization is
2. What you want to do with Meridian
3. Whether it is internal, embedded in a product, or offered as a service
4. Roughly how many people or customers it would reach

There is no published price list. Terms depend on the use, and asking costs
nothing — including asking whether you need a licence at all. If your case
turns out to be internal use, the answer is simply "you already have one".

## If you are not sure

Ask before assuming, in either direction. A question in an issue is always
welcome, and no one is doing anything wrong by asking.
