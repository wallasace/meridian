## Why this exists

Meridian is offered under two licences: an internal-use licence that costs
nothing, and a commercial licence for uses outside it. For the maintainer to
include your contribution in **both**, he needs your permission to do so.

Without that permission, a contribution could only ever appear in one of them,
which would fragment the project.

write. In that case you need their authorization before submitting, and by

The project cannot verify this and does not try to. It remains between you and
your employer.
